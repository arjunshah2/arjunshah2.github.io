About

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at King's Business School, King's College London. My research interests include economic measurement, economic diversification, knowledge spillovers and the economics of innovation.

Before joining King's, I was a Research Assistant for the Programme on Innovation and Diffusion at the Centre for Economic Performance, LSE. I hold an MSc in Economics and Social Sciences from Bocconi University.
